英文內文：======================

"Turn Every Paper You Read into a Building Block of Your Research Knowledge"

PaperReader** is a free, open-source tool designed for graduate students and academic researchers to read, annotate, and organize research papers.

https://educatres.github.io/PaperReader/

Every meaningful passage from a research paper can become a knowledge note.

PaperReader allows you to turn excerpts from research papers, AI-generated insights, and your own thoughts into notes on an **infinite knowledge whiteboard**. By freely arranging and connecting these notes, you can link ideas across different papers and gradually develop your own research framework and knowledge structure.
PaperReader integrates **PDF highlighting, screenshot-based notes, AI-powered translation, AI summarization, and full-paper overviews**, bringing paper reading, note-taking, and cross-paper knowledge integration together in one platform.
Every note can be traced back to its original source. All data is stored locally on your computer, with backup support to protect your privacy and give you full control over your research materials.
Go beyond simply highlighting papers. Make every reading session and every idea traceable, and gradually build your own personal research knowledge base.

 Access and API Keys
PaperReader is available to everyone, including users outside Chang Gung University (CGU).
However, please do not share your CGU AI API key with anyone outside the university.
Students and researchers from other institutions, as well as CGU users who have exhausted their AI token quota, can use a free API key provided by Google AI Studio.
